# HALEPA Support

[Українською](support.html)

## Quick Party Nearby check

1. Turn on Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on every iPhone.
2. Allow HALEPA to access the Local Network.
3. Keep the host room open. On the other phones, open Party Nearby and choose
   `Join`.
4. If the room does not appear, close and reopen discovery, then check
   `Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network`.

HALEPA does not require internet access or a separate server. One Phone mode runs
entirely on one iPhone.

## Report a problem

Open an issue in [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Vaniawl/partygame-pages/issues)
and include the iOS version, iPhone model, game mode, and reproduction steps. Do
not include private player names or answers.
