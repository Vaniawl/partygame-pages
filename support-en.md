# HALEPA Support

[Українська](support.html) · English

## Quick checks for Nearby play

1. Turn on Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth on every iPhone.
2. Allow HALEPA to access the local network.
3. Keep the host’s room screen open, and tap **Join** on the other phones.
4. If the room does not appear, close the search and open it again. Check
   **Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network**.

HALEPA does not require an internet connection or a separate server. **One phone** mode
runs entirely on one iPhone.

## Report a problem

Email [idovhosheia@gmail.com](mailto:idovhosheia@gmail.com) with your iOS version, iPhone
model, HALEPA version, game mode and the steps that led to the problem. Do not include
private player names or answers.

If the app closed unexpectedly, include the approximate time and describe the
action you were taking immediately before it closed.

The usual time for an initial reply is up to five working days. For Nearby play issues,
include how many iPhones were involved and whether Local Network permission was granted.
